## Executive summary

The source is cổ phong chí quái: a modern student awakens in a dead youth in a six-century dynasty where ghosts, ritual specialists and a hidden state occult institution are real. A mysterious visualization scroll plus a ghost-consuming ability creates a double-edged progression engine: power comes from dangerous contact with the dead, while sincere public belief can unlock divine inheritances.

Reader promise: each local ghost case resolves a concrete victim-level injustice while revealing a larger layer of conspiracy. Progression runs on three linked tracks: cultivation/power, investigation/knowledge, and relationships/institutional trust. Emotional rhythm alternates body horror, moral anger, punishment payoff, and family/comedic relief.

## Narrative DNA

- Genre/subgenre: chí quái cổ phong; điều tra quỷ án; tu hành tiến cấp; hành động trừ tà; institutional conspiracy.
- Functional mix: ~40% folk-horror investigation, 25% cultivation/power system, 20% action/exorcism, 10% domestic comedy, 5% romance/politics.
- Central fantasy: a storyteller/divine-medium converts knowledge, authored stories and sincere social trust into supernatural power, then uses that power to protect the same community.
- Story engine:
  1. A supernatural anomaly harms a concrete victim.
  2. The protagonist collects clues through records, customs, ghost memories, divination or experts.
  3. Investigation reveals the visible ghost is part of a larger injustice or scheme.
  4. The protagonist chooses to intervene despite fear.
  5. Combat requires a new ability/application with a cost.
  6. Local payoff leaves an object, statement or culprit leading to the next layer.
- Chapter loop: current danger → discovery/test → balance shifts → state change → new question/enemy/object/action.
- Arc loop: local disaster → victim history → occult mechanism → gather tools/allies → exorcism climax → reward/loss → evidence opening larger case.
- Retention formula: visible case + sympathetic victim + supernatural rule with cost + intelligence/preparation + moral payoff + clue to larger conspiracy.
- Escalation: one victim → household → village → province/state → underworld/world.

## Story Bible — source facts

### Premise / starting state
- C001: modern student soul enters a dead youth in the current dynasty; a visualization scroll exists in the sea of consciousness and reacts to ghosts/incense.
- Starting state: poor, physically weak, no cultivation, only modern memories, a dead mentor's legacy and poorly understood supernatural hunger.
- C100: protagonist remains realm 2 `Trăm Ngày Quan`, is an external agent of the righteous occult institution, uses anti-ghost sword, ghost-consuming ability, heavenly fire and guardian method; has passed a field test for the ninth hidden seat but is not confirmed as seated.

### Hard rules
- Sincere belief matters; self/hired worship is not equivalent to genuine faith; publication/story circulation can accumulate sincere incense.
- Ghost consumption grants energy/memory but transmits resentment and risks identity loss/overload.
- Over-level power has costs; strong weapons/techniques can fail or self-harm when cultivation is insufficient.
- Rituals require media/conditions such as talismans, icons, birth data, steps, chants and timing.
- Ghost does not equal evil; dead entities can retain loyalty and affection.
- Supernatural sight is not omniscient.
- Killing underworld soldiers can create a soul mark causing recurring pursuit; a paper-substitute tradition can transfer the mark under confirmed conditions.

### Historical backbone
- Ancient prior dynasty/civilization lasts about 1,300 years in character testimony and declines amid ghost crisis.
- ~600 years before C001: current dynasty founded; founding national preceptor/director establishes the occult institution.
- ~600 years before C001: founding director destroys an ancient ghost sect and a Tai-level ghost king; the future long-arc antagonist survives after rescue attributed to hidden leader.
- A later underworld-soldier massacre causes the founding director to enter U Minh for 49 days with a national artifact; he returns aged and underworld soldiers quiet for centuries.
- Founding director later dies; public record says old age but true file is sealed.
- 70–80 years before the dragon episode: wounded dragon falls in the province, locals save it; later becomes a local divine presence and eventually disappears/dies.
- Lân Đức 17: hidden leader + Tai-level threat attack the military noble house, kill 176, cripple the Duke, kill the main female ally's twin and badly injure the current director.
- >20 years before the later field exam: underworld soldiers cross the exam county and kill nearly one-third of >1,400 residents.
- Years before C001: long-arc antagonist prepares five-element body/ghost scheme.
- Shortly before C001: original protagonist is killed and soul extracted; modern soul wakes in the corpse.
