# World — Hương Hỏa Trấn Quỷ

## World lock
World ontology, social logic, technology level and historical backbone inherit from the source profile. Identity Remap changes proper nouns only where necessary.

## Political frame
- Dynasty: **Đại Ninh**; six-century dynastic function retained.
- Opening era: **Thừa Bình thứ bảy**.
- The dynasty is roughly six centuries old at C001.
- Supernatural danger exists but is not ordinary public governance; the imperial occult institution handles it with partial secrecy and specialized records.
- The capital contains headquarters of **Trấn Tà Giám** and a protective national formation.
- No modern technology, firearms, industrial infrastructure or game-interface assumptions.

## Society and class
- Ancient agrarian dynasty with county/provincial/capital bureaucracy, wealthy houses, noble military lineages, temples, markets, inns, printing/book trade and sex-work institutions.
- Social injustice can generate supernatural consequences but is not reduced to metaphor; occult rules are materially real.
- Wealth/status can hide crime, buy ritual help or shape records but do not guarantee supernatural safety.
- Trấn Tà Giám agents form a dangerous specialized profession with hierarchy, mortality and merit accounting.

## Economy / resources
- Conventional currency remains premodern money; source price contradictions are not made hard canon unless needed.
- Specialized economy: **thiện công**, talismans, occult materials, medicines, rare treasures, weapons, pills, spirit resources.
- Social-belief economy: sincere hương hỏa is real but cannot be purchased as a direct substitute for faith.
- Book publishing is both commercial/social network and route to sincere belief for Hoài Chân.

## Culture / belief / taboo
- Folk worship, ancestor/tablet practices, temples, talismans, exorcism customs and local legends shape interpretations of danger.
- Characters may hold wrong/incomplete/politically filtered beliefs or histories.
- Dead souls can retain moral agency; death may lead to ghost, curse, weaponization, divine role or underworld conscription.
- Human sacrifice, corpse manipulation, soul-locking and turning people into ritual material are core violations.

## Supernatural ontology
- Threat ladder: Du hồn (often outside ladder) → Quỷ → Hung → Sát → Tai → Uyên.
- Cultivator ladder is separate; no universal 1:1 conversion.
- Ghosts may retain memory/affection or be distorted by resentment.
- Divine manifestations can be reached through specific belief/ritual mechanics but require source-backed preparation/cost.
- U Phủ/âm binh are a material underworld force; exact meta-history remains partly uncertain by C100.
- Đi Âm Nhân have a separate inherited/awakened tradition with costs including Ngũ Tệ Tam Khuyết/death qi.

## Historical backbone — protected
1. **Đại Ung** predates current dynasty; character testimony says it lasted ~1,300 years and declined amid ghost catastrophe.
2. ~600 years before C001, **Triệu Huyền Đình** founds Đại Ninh; **Kỷ Thiên Cơ** becomes first national preceptor/chief director.
3. Around same era, Kỷ Thiên Cơ destroys **Huyền Âm Sơn** and a Tai-level ghost king; **Bùi Huyền** survives through intervention attributed to Thượng Tôn.
4. In **Ninh Nguyên 21**, after underworld soldiers massacre a city in **Túc Châu**, Kỷ Thiên Cơ enters U Minh for 49 days carrying **Cửu Vực Đỉnh**, returns aged; soldiers then quiet for centuries.
5. Kỷ Thiên Cơ later dies; public history says natural death but record is sealed.
6. 70–80 years before dragon episode, wounded golden dragon falls in Tĩnh Châu and is helped by locals; later becomes local divine presence, then disappears/dies; Ngọc Long confirms elder's death.
7. **Cảnh Đức 17:** Thượng Tôn and Tai-level threat attack Trấn Quốc Công residence, kill 176, cripple Duke, kill Chiêu Nguyệt's twin and severely injure Kỷ Vân Sơn.
8. >20 years before An Lăng arc, underworld soldiers pass through An Lăng and kill nearly one-third of a population above 1,400.
9. Years before C001, Tống Mù/Bùi Huyền builds five-element body/ghost plan.
10. Shortly before C001, original Hoài Chân is killed and soul extracted; modern soul wakes in corpse.

Order/results/numeric anchors are protected; names are identity remaps.

## World mysteries not to resolve early

- exact sides/structure of U Phủ world conflict;
- origin of golden incense;
- full nature of Thượng Tôn/U Tuyền;
- exact status of Lục thúc;
- origin of Thần Tướng Đồ.

## Forbidden world inventions
- No new continent/cosmology to expand scale.
- No hidden immortal realm, demon race, bloodline clan or extra dynasty without later source/user support.
- No modernization of state/economy.
- No rewrite of protected history solely to create novelty.
