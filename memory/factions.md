# Factions — Hương Hỏa Trấn Quỷ

## 1. Trấn Tà Giám
- Function: righteous imperial occult institution protecting civilians and investigating tà ám.
- Type: state occult bureaucracy.
- Goal: contain supernatural incidents, preserve dynasty, investigate high-level hidden threats.
- Public face: limited/partially hidden specialist authority.
- Hidden reality: dangerous work, sealed archives, short life expectancy, internal disagreement, long-running losses.
- Hierarchy — KEEP_FUNCTION: `Giám Chính → Giám Phó → Giám Hầu → Linh Đài Lang → Tư thần`; **ngoại sĩ** are external collaborators.
- Resources: archives, field officers, formations, `thiện công`, weapons, pills, contacts.
- Methods: cases, records, witnesses, ritual testing, field teams, sealed investigations.
- Strengths: institutional memory, specialists, legitimacy, pooled resources.
- Weaknesses: casualties, secrecy, limited manpower, internal factions, sealed information.
- Protagonist relation: outsider → Phùng Tĩnh contact → external agent under Chiêu Nguyệt → strategic ally.
- Genre Fit: PASS.

### Internal units
- **Trấn Yêu Các** — field/military-resource function.
- **Huyền Cơ Các** — archive/information function.
- **Đan Đỉnh Các** — pill/resource function.
No new division is added beyond profile-supported functions.

## 2. U Tuyền / Mười Thiên Can
- Function: hidden antagonistic organization connecting multi-arc mysteries.
- Type: secret underworld-aligned society with ten masked seats.
- Seat structure — protected: `Giáp → Ất → Bính → Đinh → Mậu → Kỷ → Canh → Tân → Nhâm → Quý`.
- Giáp = **Thượng Tôn**, leader.
- Tân = **Bì Tướng Chủ**, eighth seat and immediate enemy after C100.
- Former Nhâm = **Bùi Huyền/Tống Mù**; Hoài Chân later receives ninth-seat token/candidacy.
- Bính/Quý are absent at observed gathering; identities/reasons unknown.
- Total end goal: incomplete by C100; do not invent one.
- Methods: secrecy, masked titles, tests, recruitment/infiltration, individual seat crimes/schemes.
- Strengths: high-level members, historical depth, compartmentalization.
- Weakness: internal distrust/differences create exploitable cracks.
- Protagonist relation: enemy network → infiltration target → deadly examination.
- Genre Fit: PASS.

## 3. Huyền Âm Môn
- Function: ancient ghost-cultivation sect whose destruction creates six-century antagonist lineage.
- Type: historical tà đạo ghost sect.
- Historical role: destroyed by Kỷ Thiên Cơ around 600 years pre-C001; Bùi Huyền survives as remnant through Thượng Tôn intervention.
- Methods/resources: ghost cultivation, soul/body techniques, five-element ghost construction through remnant knowledge.
- Present state: not rebuilt as a new active mega-faction in C001–C100.
- Genre Fit: PASS.

## 4. U Phủ / âm binh / Đi Âm network
- Function: underworld military/labor network broadening world conflict beyond U Tuyền.
- Known: underworld soldiers can massacre/conscribe; killing them can cause soul marks/nightly pursuit; Đi Âm Nhân interact/work with them and possess special inherited techniques/costs.
- Leadership: **UNCONFIRMED** as unified faction by C100.
- Relationship to U Tuyền: uncertain/possibly part of larger historical contest; source uncertainty retained.
- Protagonist relation: rescue debt via Lục thúc; later direct survival conflict in An Lăng.
- Genre Fit: PASS.

## 5. Trấn Quốc Công phủ
- Function: military aristocratic house anchoring Chiêu Nguyệt's power/resources/blood feud.
- Type: founding noble/military lineage.
- Historical wound: Cảnh Đức 17 attack by Thượng Tôn/Tai-level threat kills 176, cripples Duke, kills Chiêu Nguyệt's twin and injures Kỷ Vân Sơn.
- Resources: status, equipment, martial inheritance, political reach.
- Protagonist relation: indirect → allied through Chiêu Nguyệt.
- Genre Fit: PASS.

## 6. Huyền Đỉnh Cung / divine-incense inheritance
- Function: lost old lineage tied to cultivation images/divine transmission.
- Type: historical cultivation heritage, not a newly active mega-faction.
- Retained: three first cultivation images correspond to three first realms; surviving transmission intersects Hoài Chân progression.
- Present organization status: source does not support inventing a large living sect.
- Genre Fit: PASS.
