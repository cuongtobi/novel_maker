# Style Guide — Hương Hỏa Trấn Quỷ

This guide clones **technique**, not sentences, catchphrases, chants, jokes or signature prose.

## Narrative voice
- POV: close third person, primarily Tạ Hoài Chân.
- Alternate POV: short/functional, mainly around high-pressure events; no parallel omniscient narrator.
- Distance: close; thoughts/fear/body sensation may be direct.
- Time: mostly linear past.
- Tone: folk horror + investigation + practical cultivation + restrained modern self-commentary.

| Speed | 8/10 |

These are relative profile scores, not mechanical quotas.

## Sentence / paragraph rhythm
- Action: short clauses and 1–3-sentence paragraphs when tempo spikes.
- Investigation: medium sentences anchored to observable evidence.
- Exposition: longer but broken by question/reaction/test/immediate use.
- Avoid lore walls before reader encounters consequence.
- Do not imitate source sentence openings/closings verbatim.

## Dialogue
- High density; use dialogue for rule explanation, bargaining, conflict and relationship pressure.
- Hoài Chân: modern/self-deprecating internal register; cooler/shorter when playing mysterious master.
- A Ninh: direct, naive semantic mismatch; fully new jokes.
- Chiêu Nguyệt: concise, command-like, affection via actions.
- Hàn Liệt: terse, military.
- Tống Mù: falsely intimate warmth against cruelty.
- Ngọc Long: flat/literal, calm to discomfort.
- Vệ Nhị: boastful/bargaining/sarcastic.
- Differentiate voice by rhythm/decision style, not copied catchwords.

## Exposition recipe
`encounter consequence → define rule → history/example only as needed → test/apply immediately → state cost/exception`

No encyclopedia dump detached from current objective.

## Opening patterns
1. Continue directly from prior cliffhanger consequence.
2. Ordinary domestic/public detail with one wrong element.
3. Brief enemy/secondary POV showing pressure.
4. Object/record/witness introducing actionable anomaly.

Do not repeat the same opening skeleton across a batch.

## Closing patterns
Close at genuine state change:
- identity reclassified;
- object speaks/activates;
- deadline begins;
- rescue/enemy arrives;
- old interpretation becomes impossible;
- character commits to action already underway.

Avoid fake omniscient “little did he know” hooks.

## Investigation technique
Three layers:
1. rumor/folk explanation;
2. record/witness/institutional account;
3. experienced truth via evidence, memory or confrontation.

Each layer revises the prior layer rather than deleting continuity.

## Horror technique
- Bodies/objects may become uncanny material, but horror should reveal human/ritual violation.
- Favor one or two concrete sensory details with investigative value over decorative gore.
- Social harm and supernatural mechanics reinforce each other.
- Relief comes through character behavior, not by declaring danger funny.

## Action technique
`read battlefield/rule → familiar tool partly fails → tactical shift using planted knowledge/resource → real cost → decisive visual payoff → aftermath`

- Major combat clusters can span 2–5 chapters.
- Do not solve every fight with larger realm/beam.
- Power accounting/injuries persist.

## Cultivation technique
Focused cultivation identifies:
- input/resource;
- method;
- bodily/spiritual effect;
- restriction/failure mode;
- resulting state delta.

No instant unsupported breakthrough for protected landmarks.

## Emotion
- Show care through choices, material protection, waiting, home routines, memorial acts and willingness to share risk.
- Losses alter later decisions.
- Romantic implication remains slow/behavior-based through C100; no forced label.

## Comedy
- Use character mismatch, literal misunderstanding, over-serious behavior in mundane context.
- New wording only.
- Never copy exact idiom mistakes, food-rank jokes, sexual misunderstanding patterns, book-sending jokes or chapter-urging jokes from source.
- Reduce comedy immediately after permanent loss until emotional acknowledgment occurs.

## Tension
Use source-backed:
- concrete clocks;
- countable resource limits;
- asymmetric information;
- prepared objects whose meaning changes;
- separation from strongest ally.
No arbitrary timer may be invented for a major beat.

## Audio / Vietnamese readability
- Natural Vietnamese syntax; avoid raw machine-translation inversions.
- Prefer clear referents over stacked pronouns.
- Dialogue tags only when speaker ambiguous.
- Break long exposition into speech/action/reaction units for TTS.
- Preserve locked proper nouns.
- Avoid isolated Chinese characters and source translation-name variants.

## Do-not-copy list
Do **not** directly reuse:
- source prose sentences or chapter-end wording;
- source chants/ritual wording;
- source catchphrases/repeated threats;
- source running jokes;
- iconic source-deity descriptions;
- distinctive source similes/metaphors;
- translation errors/name variants as if style.

## Drift checks
- Pure xianxia tournament/power fantasy: FAIL genre.
- Modern slang dominating ancient atmosphere: FAIL genre.
- All explanations before phenomena: FAIL source technique.
- Same cliffhanger repeated through batch: FAIL hook diversity.
- Exact source joke/chant phrasing reappears: FAIL transformation layer.
