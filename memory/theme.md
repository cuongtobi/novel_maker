# Theme — Hương Hỏa Trấn Quỷ

## Central theme
**Sức mạnh chỉ chính đáng khi người sở hữu chấp nhận nghĩa vụ với những người yếu hơn, thay vì biến họ thành vật liệu.**

## Subthemes
1. **Identity through action:** body/soul mismatch does not settle who Hoài Chân is; choices, debts and responsibility do.
2. **Chosen family:** A Ninh becomes family through repeated care, not blood.
3. **The dead preserve truth:** ghosts/memories/wills/graves can carry testimony that human records erase or falsify.
4. **Institutions are necessary but costly:** the righteous occult bureau protects through procedure/resources yet survives on risk, sacrifice and imperfect judgment.
5. **Knowledge has a price:** deeper history/power creates new obligations and danger.
6. **Stories create social reality:** belief/narrative can bind communities, become power and impose responsibility on the teller.

## Counter-theme
**Kẻ mạnh có quyền dùng kẻ yếu như nguyên liệu, bởi kết quả/quyền lực biện minh cho phương tiện.**

## Moral questions
- Does dangerous power create a duty to intervene?
- What makes a person “the same person” when body, soul and memory diverge?
- Can a state institution use secrecy without becoming indifferent to individuals?
- When a ghost harmed others after being victimized, what does justice require?
- How much can one borrow from the dead—power, memory, authority—without losing self?
- When does preparation/manipulation for a good end cross into treating people as tools?

## Reward logic
Narrative tends to reward:
- keeping promises/debts;
- preparing instead of relying on raw scale;
- protecting civilians/companions;
- judging people by conduct rather than category;
- gratitude/reciprocity;
- truth that restores victim dignity.

## Punishment logic
Narrative tends to punish:
- greed/instrumentalization;
- sacrificing weaker people for advancement;
- exploitation where it feeds the case;
- evasion of consequences;
- arrogance that mistakes long planning/power for moral entitlement.

## Motif functions — keep function, refresh wording
- **Bridge / gate:** living↔dead, ignorance↔knowledge, local case↔larger conspiracy. New surfaces: Bạch Thạch Kiều, Âm Quan.
- **Fire / water:** cleansing vs drowning, extreme-yang vs yin spaces; also concrete mechanics.
- **Head / face / skin:** identity, testimony, substitution and hidden self.
- **Incense / story / book:** faith as social bond and mechanical resource.
- **Eating vs shared meals:** devouring as dangerous acquisition contrasted with ordinary meals as family/social trust. Do not reuse source food-title jokes.
- **Tablet / will / grave / record:** the dead as evidence and institutional memory.

## Thematic guardrails
- Horror remains connected to human stakes, not gore for scale alone.
- Comedy does not erase recent death/suffering.
- Power progression increases responsibility/exposure, not only dominance.
- No new “chosen one by bloodline/system” theme.
- Do not make all institutions evil or all ghosts innocent; ambiguity is case-specific.
