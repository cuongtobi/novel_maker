- No new power system is designed.

## 1. Sources of power
1. **Cultivation / Đăng Tiên ladder** — structured internal progression.
2. **Thần Tướng Đồ + hương hỏa** — sincere belief can fill/activate divine inheritance pages.
3. **Phệ Quỷ** — consuming ghosts converts supernatural material into benefit/memory with resentment backlash.
4. **Ritual / talisman / artifact practice** — media, timing, steps and materials matter.
5. **Institutional resources** — merit, pills, equipment, archives and training.
6. **Ghost-command branch** — A Ninh's later authority and troops.
7. **Đi Âm tradition** — separate occult inheritance with bodily/fate costs.

## 5. Divine-page identity lock
- First-page divine identity: **Huyền Diện Trấn Quỷ Chân Quân** — ghost-judge/exorcism function.
- Second-page divine identity: **Xích Tiêu Hộ Pháp Chân Quân** — fiery guardian function.

Mechanics, reveal timing, moral function and costs stay locked to profile. No extra mythology/lineage may be attached to these remapped identities.

## 6. Breakthrough rules
- Cultivation must have source-backed input, practice, condition and time.
- Realm 1/2 occur at protected beats; realm 3 remains future after C100.
- Pills/resources can support, not erase, requirements.
- New divine inheritance appears only at source-equivalent setup/payoff timing.
- A later technique cannot appear before its source anchor.

## 7. Costs / failure modes
- Phệ Quỷ: resentment/memory contamination and overload.
- Sincere belief: cannot be manufactured by self-worship alone.
- Powerful sword: may be unusable before sufficient state.
- Xích Tiêu Thiên Hỏa: can burn Hoài Chân himself.
- Hộ Pháp Quyết: large mana expenditure.
- Body-lock cultivation: behavioral/physical restrictions.
- Rituals: wrong/missing media can fail.
- Đi Âm: Ngũ Tệ Tam Khuyết/death qi and pursuit obligations.
- Underworld-soldier combat: soul mark and repeated pursuit.

## 8. Tactical/counter logic
- Exorcism is rule-based, not pure damage scaling.
- Information, ritual preparation, environmental positioning, faith, poison, substitutes or formation disruption can matter.
- Familiar tool can fail against a new rule, forcing a source-backed tactical shift.
- No absolute immunity unless source confirms it.
